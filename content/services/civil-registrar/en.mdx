<Topic id="lcro" theme="birth" icon="building" title="City and Municipal Civil Registrars" subtitle="Where births, marriages and deaths are registered">

### Who keeps civil registry records

Civil registration is a city and municipal function. Each of Bukidnon's two cities, Malaybalay and Valencia, and each of its 20 municipalities has its own Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO), headed by the city or municipal civil registrar. A birth, marriage or death is registered with the LCRO of the place where it happened. The Provincial Government of Bukidnon does not register these events or issue civil registry documents.

#### What the civil registrar does

<Checklist>

- Registers births, marriages and deaths
- Issues marriage licenses
- Issues certified copies of records on file
- Accepts petitions to correct records
- Handles delayed registration
- Sends records to the PSA

</Checklist>

#### Getting a copy from the civil registrar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the right office">
    Go to the civil registrar of the city or municipality where the event was registered. Use the
    list of cities and municipalities to find it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring a valid ID">
    Bring a valid government-issued ID. A representative also brings an authorization letter and the
    document owner's ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill out the request form">
    Give the names on the record and the date and place of the event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay and claim">
    Pay the fee set by the city or municipality and claim your certified copy. Fees and processing
    times differ from one LGU to another.
  </Step>
</Steps>

[Find your city or municipality](/government#lgus)

</Topic>

<Topic id="psa" theme="cenomar" icon="globe" title="PSA Copies" subtitle="Certified copies on security paper from the Philippine Statistics Authority">

### When you need a PSA copy

Civil registrars send their records to the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), which keeps the national copy. PSA copies are printed on security paper and are usually required for passports, visas, and many national government transactions. Only the PSA issues the Certificate of No Marriage Record (CENOMAR).

#### Ways to request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Online through PSA Serbilis">
    Order online, pay the PSA fee, and have the copy delivered to your address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="At a PSA outlet">
    Visit a PSA Civil Registry System outlet with a valid ID. Check the PSA website for outlet
    locations and schedules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your civil registrar">
    Some city and municipal civil registrars accept requests for PSA copies. Ask your local office
    if it offers this.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the PSA has no copy of a record that was registered locally, ask the civil registrar where it was registered to endorse it to the PSA.

[PSA Serbilis (online requests)](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/)

</Topic>

<Topic id="correction" theme="correction" icon="pencil-square" title="Corrections and Amendments" subtitle="Fixing errors in birth, marriage and death records">

### About Corrections

Under Republic Act 9048 and Republic Act 10172, some errors can be corrected by petition at the civil registrar, without going to court. File the petition with the civil registrar of the city or municipality where the record is registered. If you now live somewhere else, you may be able to file it with the civil registrar where you live. Changes these laws do not cover need a court order.

#### What can be corrected by petition

<Checklist>

- Clerical or typographical errors (RA 9048)
- Change of first name (RA 9048)
- Day or month of birth (RA 10172)
- Sex, when the error is clerical (RA 10172)

</Checklist>

Filing fees, publication and supporting documents depend on the type of petition. Ask the civil registrar for the current requirements.

</Topic>

<Topic id="delayed" theme="delayed" icon="clock-history" title="Delayed Registration" subtitle="Late registration of birth, marriage, or death">

### About Delayed Registration

A birth or death that was not registered within 30 days, or a marriage that was not registered within the required period, can still be registered through delayed registration. File it with the civil registrar of the city or municipality where the event happened.

#### Commonly asked for

<Checklist>

- PSA negative certification
- Affidavit explaining the delay
- Supporting documents for the event
- Valid IDs

</Checklist>

</Topic>
