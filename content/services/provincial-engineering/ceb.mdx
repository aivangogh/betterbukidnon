<Section alt icon="bank" title="Mahitungod sa Opisina" intro="Unsay buhat sa Provincial Engineer ubos sa balaod">

<Prose>

The Provincial Engineer's Office (PEO) is the engineering and public works office of the Province of Bukidnon. Under the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160), the Provincial Engineer plans, designs, supervises and maintains the infrastructure of the province, such as provincial roads, bridges and buildings, and gives technical help to the cities and municipalities.

The PEO does not handle every road or building in Bukidnon. Building permits, local streets and barangay roads belong to your city or municipality, and national roads belong to the DPWH.

</Prose>

<Section alt icon="list-ol" title="Unsaon Pagkontak ang Opisina" intro="Kinatibuk-ang mga lakang. Tan-awa ang Citizen's Charter alang sa opisyal nga mga lakang.">

<Columns>

<Box title="Mga Lakang">

1. Find out who maintains the road or facility. Provincial roads are the PEO's concern; national roads, city or municipal streets and barangay roads are not.
2. Call the PEO or visit the office at the Capitol. Describe the location and the problem, with a landmark or a photo if you have one.
3. Pangutana kung unsaon pag-asikaso ang imong report o hangyo ug kung kinahanglan ba kini og sulat.
4. Tipigi ang ngalan sa tawo nga imong nakigsulti ug ang petsa, kung kinahanglan nimo mag-follow up.

</Box>

<Box title="Unsay Dad-on" note="Mahimong lahi ang mga rekisitos; kumpirmaha sa opisina.">

- Balidong government-issued ID
- Eksaktong lokasyon, uban ang landmark, sa dalan, tulay, o proyekto
- Sinulat nga sulat alang sa pormal nga mga hangyo, sama sa teknikal nga tabang

</Box>
