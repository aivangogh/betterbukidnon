<Section alt icon="bank" title="Mahitungod sa Opisina" intro="Unsay buhat sa Provincial Accountant ubos sa balaod">

<Prose>

The Provincial Accountant's Office keeps the books of the Provincial Government of Bukidnon. Under the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160), the provincial accountant sets up and maintains the Province's accounting system, reviews the documents behind every payment before it is released (pre-audit), certifies that funds are available for an expense, and prepares the financial statements and reports required by law.

The office works with the Provincial Treasurer, the Provincial Budget Office and the Commission on Audit (COA). It serves the Province's own offices first. Members of the public usually deal with it as suppliers, contractors, employees or other payees of the Province.

</Prose>

<Section icon="list-check" title="Unsay Buhat sa Opisina" intro="Kinatibuk-ang paghulagway sa mga serbisyo niini. Tan-awa ang Citizen's Charter alang sa opisyal nga lista.">

<Cards>

<Card icon="people" title="Alang sa Publiko">

- Answers questions from suppliers, contractors and other payees about the status of a claim for payment.
- Nag-pre-audit sa mga claim ug supporting documents sa wala pa buhian ang bayad.
- Certifies copies of accounting records when a request is allowed under the Province's rules.

</Card>

<Card icon="diagram-3" title="Alang sa Ubang Opisina sa Probinsya">

- Reviews disbursement vouchers, payrolls and supporting documents for completeness and legality.
- Nagsertipika nga adunay pondo sa wala pa aprubahan ang pagpalit o kontrata.
- Nagtala sa mga resibo, obligasyon, ug bayad, ug nagtipig sa mga libro sa accounts.
- Nag-andam og mga financial statement ug ulat para sa COA, DBM ug sa Sangguniang Panlalawigan.

</Card>

<Card icon="buildings" title="Alang sa mga Siyudad ug Munisipyo">

- Naghatag og kinatibuk-ang giya sa mga lagda sa accounting kung hangyoon.
- Each city and municipality keeps its own books through its own accountant. The Province does not process municipal claims.

</Card>

</Cards>

<Section alt icon="list-ol" title="Unsaon Pag-follow Up sa Claim alang sa Bayad" intro="Kinatibuk-ang mga lakang. Tan-awa ang Citizen's Charter alang sa opisyal nga mga lakang.">

<Columns>

<Box title="Mga Lakang">

1. Prepare the claim and its supporting documents, and file them with the provincial office that requested your goods or services.
2. That office prepares the disbursement voucher and sends it to the Accountant's Office for pre-audit.
3. If a document is missing or incorrect, the Accountant's Office returns the voucher to the requesting office. Complete the document and resubmit.
4. After pre-audit the voucher goes on to the Treasurer for payment. Ask the requesting office about its status, or call the Capitol main line.

</Box>

<Box title="Unsay Dad-on">

- Balidong government-issued ID
- Delivery receipts, billing statement, kontrata o purchase order, kung magamit
- Imong mga dokumento sa buhis ug pagrehistro sa negosyo, kung supplier o contractor ka
- Kung representante ka: sulat sa pagtugot ug ID sa payee

</Box>

</Columns>
