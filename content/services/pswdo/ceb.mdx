<Prose>

The Provincial Social Welfare and Development Office (PSWDO) delivers the social welfare programs of the Province of Bukidnon. Under the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160), the provincial social welfare and development officer develops and carries out programs and services for the welfare of the poor and vulnerable, including children, youth, women, senior citizens, persons with disability, and indigenous peoples.

The PSWDO works with the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) and with the social welfare offices of the cities and municipalities. For many walk-in services, those local offices are your first stop.

</Prose>

<Section icon="heart" title="Mga Programa ug Serbisyo" intro="Provincial programs in general terms. Many walk-in services start at your city or municipal social welfare office.">

<Cards>

<Card
  icon="cash-coin"
  title="Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)"
  tag="Tabang sa krisis"
>
  Help for people in a crisis, such as medical, burial, transportation or food needs. Residents
  usually apply at the social welfare office of their city or municipality, and may be referred to
  the PSWDO.
</Card>

<Card icon="person-heart" title="Mga Senior Citizen" tag="Mga senior citizen">
  Support and referral for senior citizens. Senior citizen IDs and the social pension are processed
  through the city or municipal offices and the national DSWD.
</Card>

<Card icon="person-wheelchair" title="Mga Persons with Disability" tag="PWD">
  Provincial support for persons with disability and their families. PWD IDs are applied for at the
  city or municipal office.
</Card>

<Card icon="gender-female" title="Mga Babaye ug Bata" tag="Mga babaye ug bata">
  Protective services and referral for women and children, including abuse cases. In an emergency
  call 911 or the police first.
</Card>

<Card icon="people" title="Kaayohan sa mga Katawhang Lumad" tag="Mga katawhang lumad">
  Support for the welfare of Bukidnon's indigenous peoples: the Bukidnon, Higaonon, Talaandig,
  Manobo, Matigsalug, Tigwahanon and Umayamnon.
</Card>

<Card icon="house-heart" title="Tabang Human sa Katalagman" tag="Apektadong mga pamilya">
  The PSWDO works with the Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Department (PDRRMD) on
  relief for affected families.
</Card>

</Cards>

<Section alt icon="list-ol" title="Unsaon Paghangyo og Tabang" intro="Kinatibuk-ang mga lakang. Tan-awa ang Citizen's Charter alang sa opisyal nga mga lakang.">

<Columns>

<Box title="Mga Lakang">

1. Go first to the social welfare office of your city or municipality. Most walk-in services, such as assistance and IDs, begin there.
2. Ask whether the PSWDO is the right office for your case, or whether you will be referred to it.
3. Ipatin-aw ang imong sitwasyon sa social worker, nga mo-interview kanimo ug mag-assess sa imong panginahanglan.
4. Submit your documents and follow the office's instructions on any referral letter or next step.

</Box>

<Box title="Unsay Dad-on" note="Mahimong lahi ang mga rekisitos; kumpirmaha sa opisina.">

- Balidong government-issued ID
- Proof of the need, such as a medical certificate, hospital bill, death certificate or incident report
- Barangay certificate o certificate of indigency, kung gipangayo sa opisina
- Alang sa representante: sulat sa pagtugot ug ID sa kliyente

</Box>
