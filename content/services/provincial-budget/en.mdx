<Section alt icon="bank" title="About the Office" intro="What the Provincial Budget Officer does under the law">

<Prose>

The Provincial Budget Office helps the Governor prepare the Province's annual budget and keeps track of how it is carried out. Under the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160), the provincial budget officer takes charge of budget preparation, reviews the budget proposals of provincial offices, and advises on budget matters, including the supplemental budgets that the Sangguniang Panlalawigan may enact.

During the year the office records appropriations, allotments and obligations and checks that spending stays within the approved budget. It works with the Provincial Planning and Development Office, the Provincial Treasurer and the Provincial Accountant. The Sangguniang Panlalawigan approves the budget through the appropriation ordinance.

</Prose>

<Section icon="list-check" title="What the Office Does" intro="General description of its services. See the Citizen's Charter for the official list.">

<Cards>

<Card icon="people" title="To the Public">

- Explains in general terms how the Province prepares and approves its budget.
- Helps residents find where the approved budget and related public documents are posted.
- Refers budget questions about a specific project to the office that implements it.

</Card>

<Card icon="diagram-3" title="To Other Provincial Offices">

- Issues the budget call and guides offices in preparing their proposals.
- Reviews proposals and consolidates them into the Executive Budget.
- Processes requests for allotment and obligation, and checks them against the appropriation.
- Prepares reports on budget use for the Governor and the Sangguniang Panlalawigan.

</Card>

<Card icon="buildings" title="To Cities and Municipalities">

- Gives general technical guidance on local budgeting when asked.
- The Sangguniang Panlalawigan reviews the appropriation ordinances of the component cities and municipalities as the law provides. Each city and municipality prepares its own budget through its own budget officer.

</Card>

</Cards>

<Section alt icon="list-ol" title="How to Raise a Budget Question" intro="General steps. See the Citizen's Charter for the official steps.">

<Columns>

<Box title="Steps">

1. Find out which provincial office is responsible for the program or project you are asking about.
2. Ask that office first. Program offices know the status and the funds assigned to their projects.
3. For questions about the Province's overall budget, contact the Budget Office through the Capitol main line.
4. To see documents that were already made public, check bukidnon.gov.ph or send a Freedom of Information request. Ask the office how to file one.

</Box>

<Box title="What to Bring">

- A valid government-issued ID
- The name of the program, project or ordinance you are asking about
- If you represent an organization: a letter or authorization from it

</Box>

</Columns>
