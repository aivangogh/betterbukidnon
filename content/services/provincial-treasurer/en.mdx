<Section alt icon="bank" title="About the Office" intro="What the Provincial Treasurer does under the law">

<Prose>

The Provincial Treasurer's Office (PTO) is the treasury of the Province of Bukidnon. Under the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160), the Provincial Treasurer takes custody of and manages provincial funds, handles the Province's disbursements, collects the taxes, fees and charges that belong to the Province, and keeps the provincial tax records.

The Provincial Treasurer is appointed by the Secretary of Finance from nominees of the Governor, and exercises technical supervision over the treasury offices of Malaybalay City, Valencia City and the 20 municipalities.

</Prose>

<Cards>

<Card

  title="Real Property Transfer Tax"
  tags={['Property owners', 'Provincial tax']}
>
  Tax on the sale, donation, inheritance or other transfer of ownership of land and buildings in the
  20 municipalities. Proof of payment is needed to register the transfer (Local Government Code,
  Sec. 135).
</Card>

<Card icon="person-badge" title="Professional Tax (PTR)" tags={['Professionals', 'Annual']}>
  Annual tax on professionals whose practice requires a government licensure exam. It is paid to the
  province or city where the professional practices or keeps a principal office (Local Government
  Code, Sec. 139).
</Card>

<Card
  icon="minecart-loaded"
  title="Sand, Gravel and Quarry Tax"
  tags={['Quarry operators', 'Provincial tax']}
>
  Tax on stones, sand, gravel, earth and other quarry resources extracted in the province. Under the
  law, quarry permits are issued by the Governor (Local Government Code, Sec. 138).
</Card>

<Card icon="ticket-perforated" title="Amusement Tax" tags={['Businesses', 'Provincial tax']}>
  Tax on operators of cinemas, theaters, concert halls, circuses and other places of amusement
  (Local Government Code, Sec. 140).
</Card>

<Card icon="truck" title="Other Provincial Taxes" tags={['Businesses', 'Provincial tax']}>
  Franchise tax, tax on printing and publishing, and the annual fixed tax on delivery trucks and
  vans of manufacturers and dealers (Local Government Code, Secs. 136, 137 and 141).
</Card>

<Card
  icon="receipt-cutoff"

  tags={['Everyone', 'Official receipt']}

  Fees for documents, permits and the use of provincial property, as set by provincial ordinance.
  Every payment gets an official receipt.

<Section alt icon="house-door" title="Real Property Tax Payment" intro="Annual tax on land, buildings and machinery, also called amilyar">

<Columns>

<Box title="Where to Pay" listIcon="geo-alt-fill" note="In the municipalities, the tax is shared by the Province, the municipality and the barangay. The municipal treasurer collects it and remits the Province's share.">

- Property in one of the 20 municipalities: pay at the municipal treasurer's office of the town where the property is located.
- Property in Malaybalay City or Valencia City: pay at the city treasurer's office. The two cities levy and collect their own real property tax.

</Box>

<Box title="Requirements" note="Requirements may vary; confirm with the treasurer's office.">

- Tax declaration number or last year's official receipt
- Valid ID of Property Owner
- Authorization Letter (if representative)

</Box>

</Columns>

<Section icon="list-ol" title="How to Pay a Provincial Tax or Fee" intro="General steps at the PTO. See the Citizen's Charter for the official steps.">

<Columns>

<Box title="Steps">

1. Go to the PTO with your documents and ask for the computation (assessment) of the tax or fee due.
2. Check the computation, then pay the amount at the cashier.
3. Get your official receipt and check that your name, the property or transaction, and the amount are correct.
4. Keep the receipt. You will need it for the next step, such as registering a transfer or getting a new tax declaration.

</Box>

<Box title="What to Bring">

- A valid government-issued ID
- Documents for the transaction, for example the deed of sale, donation or extrajudicial settlement and the tax declaration for transfer tax
- For the professional tax: your PRC ID or other proof of license
- If you are a representative: an authorization letter or special power of attorney, and the owner's ID

</Box>

</Columns>
