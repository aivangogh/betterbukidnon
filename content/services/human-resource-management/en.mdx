<Section alt icon="bank" title="About the Office" intro="What the Provincial HRMO does under the law">

<Prose>

The Provincial Human Resource Management Office (PHRMO) looks after the people who work for the Provincial Government of Bukidnon. Under the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160) and civil service rules, the office helps the Governor with recruitment, selection and placement, keeps employee records, handles benefits and leave, and runs programs on training, performance and employee welfare.

The PHRMO works within Civil Service Commission (CSC) rules. Most members of the public deal with it as job applicants, or as current or former provincial employees who need a record.

</Prose>

</Section>

<Section icon="list-check" title="What the Office Does" intro="General description of its services. See the Citizen's Charter for the official list.">

<Cards>

<Card icon="people" title="To the Public">

- Accepts applications for vacant provincial positions and posts vacancies as civil service rules require.
- Answers questions on qualifications and the hiring process for provincial positions.
- Issues certifications of employment and copies of service records to current and former provincial employees.

</Card>

<Card icon="diagram-3" title="To Other Provincial Offices">

- Handles recruitment, appointments and personnel records (the 201 file).
- Processes leave, benefits and other employee entitlements.
- Organizes training and performance management.
- Supports discipline and employee welfare programs.

</Card>

<Card icon="buildings" title="To Cities and Municipalities">

- May give general guidance on civil service rules when asked.
- City and municipal employees are managed by their own HR offices and appointing authorities. The Province does not process their appointments or records.

</Card>

</Cards>

</Section>

<Section alt icon="list-ol" title="How to Apply or Request a Record" intro="General steps. See the Citizen's Charter for the official steps.">

<Columns>

<Box title="Steps">

1. For a job: look for posted vacancies at the Capitol or on bukidnon.gov.ph, and check the required qualifications.
2. Prepare the documents listed in the posting and submit them to the PHRMO within the stated period.
3. For a certification or service record: ask the PHRMO what it needs, submit your request, and wait to be told when it is ready.
4. Keep your receipt or acknowledgment of what you submitted.

</Box>

<Box title="What to Bring">

- A valid government-issued ID
- For job applicants: the documents listed in the vacancy posting
- For records: your name as it appeared in service, your position, and the dates you served
- If you are a representative: an authorization letter and the employee's ID

</Box>

</Columns>
